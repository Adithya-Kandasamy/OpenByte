## OpenByte photographs (own material)

`public/media/` (student project, recognition moment, workshop room, Demo Day team,
coding demo, student presenters, and the founders photo featured in the Bulldog
Tribune). They are used only where the page describes OpenByte's own activity.

## Dotted map

`map-1300.webp`, `map-2000.webp`, `map-m-900.webp` are rendered from U.S. Census Bureau
state boundaries (public domain; simplified GeoJSON from the PublicaMundi MappingAPI
repository). Virginia is highlighted; the marker shows Ashburn, Virginia
(39.043° N, 77.487° W), where OpenByte is based. Generated November 3, 2026.
